💡 Eligibility Guidelines

  • Age Requirement: Minimum 18 years of age.
  • Cooldown Period: At least 90 days (3 months) must elapse between whole blood donations.
  • Minimum Weight: Must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • Hemoglobin: At least 12.5 g/dL verified at the center.
  • General Health: Well-rested, hydrated, and free from active infections or fever.
